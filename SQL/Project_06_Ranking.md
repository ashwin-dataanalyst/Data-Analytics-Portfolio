
# 📊 Project 06 — Top Customers in Every State

## 🎯 Business Problem

The management wants to identify the top-performing customers in every state.

Instead of finding the top customers across the entire company, they want to know who contributes the highest revenue within each individual state.

This information helps regional sales managers focus on their most valuable customers.

---

# 🧠 My Thought Process

Before writing SQL, I divided the problem into smaller analytical steps.

### Chronology

1. Calculate total sales generated by every customer in every state.
2. Rank customers separately inside each state.
3. Display only the Top 3 customers from every state.
4. Compare customer performance region-wise.
