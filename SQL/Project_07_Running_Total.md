
# 📊 Project 07 — Running Revenue Analysis

## 🎯 Business Problem

The management wants to monitor how revenue accumulates as products are ranked from the highest-selling to the lowest-selling.

Instead of looking at individual product sales, they want to observe cumulative revenue generation.

This analysis helps answer questions such as:

- How quickly does revenue accumulate?
- Which products contribute most to cumulative revenue?
- At what point does the company achieve the majority of its sales?

---

# 🧠 My Thought Process

Before writing SQL, I divided the problem into smaller analytical steps.

### Chronology

1. Calculate total revenue generated by every product.
2. Sort products from highest revenue to lowest revenue.
3. Assign a ranking to every product.
4. Calculate cumulative (running) revenue.
5. Display the cumulative revenue after every product.

# 📈 Business Interpretation

Running revenue shows how quickly total company revenue accumulates as products are added one by one.

Products appearing near the beginning of the ranking contribute significantly more revenue than those appearing later.

This analysis provides a foundation for inventory optimization, Pareto Analysis and strategic product prioritization.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendation

- Prioritize inventory for products generating the largest cumulative revenue.
- Focus marketing efforts on products that contribute most to business revenue.
- Regularly monitor cumulative revenue trends to optimize product strategy.
