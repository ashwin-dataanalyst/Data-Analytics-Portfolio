
# 📊 Project 03 — Pareto Analysis (80/20 Rule)

## 🎯 Business Problem

The management wants to identify how many products generate approximately **80% of the company's total revenue**.

Instead of treating every product equally, the company wants to focus on the products contributing the majority of revenue.

---

# 🧠 My Thought Process

Before writing SQL, I divided the problem into smaller analytical steps.

### Chronology

1. Calculate total revenue generated by every product.
2. Sort products from highest revenue to lowest revenue.
3. Calculate running (cumulative) revenue.
4. Calculate cumulative revenue percentage.
5. Identify the first product where cumulative percentage reaches or exceeds 80%.
6. Count the number of products required to reach the 80% revenue mark.

# 📈 Business Interpretation

The analysis shows that only a relatively small number of products generate nearly 80% of the company's revenue.

This follows the famous **Pareto Principle (80/20 Rule)**, where a minority of products contribute the majority of business value.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendation

- Prioritize inventory for high-performing products.
- Allocate more marketing budget to these products.
- Ensure these products remain in stock.
- Analyze why the remaining products contribute comparatively less.
- Consider discontinuing consistently underperforming products.
