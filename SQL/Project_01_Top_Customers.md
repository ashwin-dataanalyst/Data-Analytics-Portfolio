# 📊 Project 01 — Top Customers Revenue Contribution Analysis

## 🎯 Business Problem

The management wants to identify the top 5 customers contributing the highest revenue and determine how much they contribute to the company's total sales.

---

# 🧠 My Thought Process

Before writing SQL, I broke the problem into smaller steps.

### Chronology

1. Calculate the total sales generated by every customer.
2. Calculate the overall company sales.
3. Calculate each customer's contribution percentage.
4. Sort customers according to revenue.
5. Display only the Top 5 customers.
6. Interpret the business impact.

---

# 🛠 SQL Concepts Used

- GROUP BY
- SUM()
- Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
- Aggregate Functions
- Percentage Calculation
- ORDER BY

# 📈 Business Interpretation

The analysis identifies the customers generating the highest proportion of company revenue.

Although these customers contribute significantly to business revenue, depending heavily on a small number of customers increases business risk.

The company should also investigate why other customers contribute less and identify opportunities to improve their purchasing behavior.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendation

- Identify products frequently purchased by top customers.
- Study pricing strategies followed for these customers.
- Develop loyalty programs for medium-value customers.
- Increase the number of high-value customers instead of depending on only a few.
