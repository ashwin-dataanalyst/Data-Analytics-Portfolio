
# 📊 Project 08 — Category Performance Analysis

## 🎯 Business Problem

The management wants to identify products performing above their category average revenue.

Instead of comparing every product across the entire company, the company wants to evaluate products only within their respective categories.

This helps identify exceptional products and underperforming products in each category.

---

# 🧠 My Thought Process

Before writing SQL, I divided the problem into smaller analytical steps.

### Chronology

1. Calculate total revenue generated by every product.
2. Calculate average product revenue for every category.
3. Compare every product's revenue with its category average.
4. Display only those products performing above the category average.

# 📈 Business Interpretation

Comparing products against their category average provides a much fairer evaluation than comparing every product across the company.

A product generating ₹50,000 in one category may be outstanding, while the same revenue in another category may be average.

This analysis helps identify high-performing products within each category.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendation

- Promote products performing above category average.
- Analyze why these products outperform competitors.
- Improve pricing or marketing for below-average products.
- Allocate inventory based on category leaders.

---

# 📚 Key Learning

This project taught me how SQL can compare individual performance against group benchmarks using aggregation and joins.

I also learned that business comparisons should be made within meaningful groups rather than across unrelated categories.
